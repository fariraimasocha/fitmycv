## The angle

Every tool in this space pitches "AI resume builder". That claim is saturated and
listicle authors ignore it. FitMyCV has one genuinely differentiated mechanic:

> **You paste the job's URL, not the job's text.**

No other tool in the mainstream comparison set reads the posting from a link.
That is the sentence to lead every pitch with, because it is a *category* claim
rather than a *quality* claim — and listicle authors need categories to fill
their "best for X" slots.

Suggested slot to ask for: **"Best for tailoring to a specific job posting"**.
Do not ask for "best overall". You will not get it and asking marks the pitch as
unserious.

## Pitch template

Keep it under 120 words. Anything longer is deleted.

```
Subject: One category your [ARTICLE TITLE] list is missing

Hi [NAME],

Your [ARTICLE TITLE] piece is the one I keep seeing cited — the [SPECIFIC TOOL]
section in particular is more honest than most.

One gap: every tool on the list makes you paste the job description text.
FitMyCV takes the job's URL and reads the posting directly, which matters
because most people paste half of it and miss the requirements block.

If you're ever adding a "best for tailoring to a specific posting" slot, we'd
fit it. There's a free ATS checker at [URL] with no signup if you want to test
the matching without an account.

Either way — we published our own comparison at [URL] and listed where we're
the wrong choice, in case any of it is useful to you.

[NAME]
```

**Do not** offer payment, link exchanges, or "we'll link back to you". Both kill
the pitch with any editor worth being listed by.

## Before pitching anything

1. The comparison article must be live and indexed — an author will check it.
2. The free checkers must work without an account. They do; keep it that way.
3. Have a one-line answer ready for "how many users do you have?" A small honest
   number beats a vague one, and inflating it is how you get removed from a list
   later.
