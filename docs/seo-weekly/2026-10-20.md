## Findings

### 1. Best free ATS checkers post converts but sits at the bottom of page one
- URL: `/blog/best-free-ats-resume-checkers-2026`
- Queries: "best ats resume checker free" (28d: 79 impr, 5 clicks, 6.3% CTR, pos 10.2), "best free ats resume checker" (18 impr, pos 7.7), "atsgrader" (pos 2.1), "best free ats checker" (pos 5.4).
- Now vs before: previous week showed "best ats resume checker free" at pos 9.6 with 2 clicks from 23 impressions. This week, no query for the page reached the 5 impression floor in the GSC export. PostHog still shows 10 organic sessions this week.
- Why it matters: 21% of its organic visitors sign up, the highest rate of any content page.
- Action: refreshed this run (see below).
- Confidence: medium. Query volume is small and the weekly drop could be noise.
- Status: new.

### 2. Job application letter: lots of impressions, few clicks, no signups
- URL: `/blog/job-application-letter`
- Queries: "application letter for a job" (380 impr, 0 clicks, pos 7.0), "job application letter" (224 impr, 4 clicks, pos 5.9), "how to write application letter for a job" (178 impr, 1 click, pos 8.4), "application letter for employment" moved from 36.8 to 12.1.
- Now vs before: about 968 impressions and 5 clicks this week, vs about 649 and 5 last week. CTR is about 0.5%.
- Why it matters: it's the site's biggest impression source, but 16 organic sessions in 28 days produced no signups.
- Action: none this week. It was retitled on Oct 13 and again on Oct 17, so the current window has only two days of the new title. Check the CTR next week. If the CTR improves and signups stay at zero, give the cover letter generator a stronger in-body pitch.
- Confidence: high on the CTR problem, low on the effect of the Oct 17 change so far.
- Status: already tracked (changed Oct 13 and Oct 17, outside this loop).
